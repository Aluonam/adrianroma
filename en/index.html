Studio

Sculpture as a means of translation between the artist and the environment.

The sculptural practice of Adrián Roma (Spain) investigates the processes by which the geometric structures found in nature can be reinterpreted and carried into the language of contemporary sculpture.

Through observation, analysis and the transformation of matter, his work explores the tension between structure and organic form. Geometry is not reproduced but translated: it is altered, fragmented and rebuilt until it becomes a new reality. Each piece emerges from this process of transformation, in which matter stops being a mere support and becomes an active part in the construction of form.
